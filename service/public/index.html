What you get

A browser Codex can use, without handing it your secrets.

A browser that stays logged in

Codex gets its own browser, in the cloud or on your machine, and stays signed in to the sites you use. Bring logins over from Chrome, Arc, Brave or Edge site by site, or sign in once.

A vault Codex never reads

Cards, passwords and ID numbers go into an encrypted vault on your computer through a one-time page. Codex can type a value into a field, but the value never shows up in anything it reads.

Nothing bought without you

Codex fills the cart and stops. You approve the exact items, total, address and card. The pay button only works for that order, at that store, while that total is on screen.

The vault

You type the secret. Codex only gets to use it.

  • Stays on your computerEncrypted with AES-256-GCM and a key that never leaves your machine. The cloud browser never stores it.
  • Hidden from the modelSnapshots, page text and screenshots are redacted on vault pages and in filled fields.
  • Logins that check themselvesAfter you save a password, Codex tries it and reports verified only when the site shows you are signed in. Two-factor codes go through the same page.
  • Pasted a card in chat?Codex ignores it and sends you the vault link instead.

How it works

The browser can run anywhere. The secrets stay home.

A small service on your computer holds the vault and the approval gate. The browser runs in the cloud by default, so no window opens on your machine, and Codex drives it through that service.

Questions

Before you install

What do I need?

Codex, the app or the CLI, on macOS or Linux. Node and Playwright ship with the plugin, so there is nothing else to install.

Does it work outside Codex?

The tools are a standard MCP server, so other agents can call them. The setup and the instructions are written for Codex today.

Can Codex buy something on its own?

No. The gate checks the store, your approval and the total on screen in code, not in the prompt. A web page telling Codex to buy something is treated as text.

What does it not protect against?

Software running as you on your own computer. The vault keeps secrets away from the model and from prompt injection; it is not a sandbox. See the security notes.

Where do downloads go?

To an inskit folder inside Downloads. Codex can also attach your local files to forms when you ask.

Is it really open source?

Yes, Apache-2.0. The plugin, the hosted service and this page are all in one repository.
